03 · Modular Panels

How tools work together (without copy-pasting)

In Orthese, each tool on your screen is called a Piece. Because your Pieces share one workspace, your ideas move between them automatically.

1

Write your idea once

Draft a brief, outline, or rough prompt inside AI Prompt Box, or attach images and documents.

2

Send straight to another panel

Your structured brief flows automatically into Website Builder or Research Canvas with zero copy-paste.

3

Refine & export

Ask for visual or wording tweaks in plain English, then download your finished page or notes.
